About NEON ORBIT ∞ SINGULARITY

NEON ORBIT ∞ SINGULARITY is a free, original browser-based neon reaction and precision arcade game created by Cornelius Aurelius. Time a rotating spark inside illuminated orbital gates to build combos, unlock tougher sectors and improve your reflexes. Play using a touchscreen, mouse or keyboard — no account, installation or payment required.

Made for phones, tablets, Chromebooks and desktop computers. Gameplay runs locally in your browser; personal bests and settings are saved on your device.
